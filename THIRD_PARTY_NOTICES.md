# Third-party scientific provenance

The HRS, RAND HRS, HRS Tracker, CHARLS and Harmonized CHARLS data sources retain their own access conditions. This anonymous package does not grant rights to redistribute their microdata.

Public GEO accession numbers and CELLxGENE dataset identifiers refer to source investigations, not the authors of the present manuscript. They are retained for reproducibility. Hallmark gene sets are provided as the original Enrichr-formatted MSigDB Hallmark 2020 resource used in the analysis; retain applicable MSigDB/Enrichr attribution and resource terms when reusing it.

The generic `nature_style.py` plotting helper comes from the Nature Figure Production skill, distributed under CC-BY-4.0. It is used to preserve the existing artwork style. R and Python libraries are not vendored and retain their respective licences.
